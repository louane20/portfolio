// profile

Infrastructure that stays up, and stays out.

I'm a Computer Systems Security graduate working in DevOps, where I design and operate secure, scalable production environments end to end. My day-to-day spans Kubernetes platform engineering, infrastructure automation with Ansible, GitOps delivery with Argo CD, and network security with Cilium — often inside air-gapped and multi-datacenter environments where "it works on my machine" isn't an option.

Before DevOps, I spent two years researching Zero Trust architectures for 5G networks, which is where the security instincts come from: I don't just deploy a cluster, I think about who — or what — is allowed to talk to it, and why. I work across development, security, network and infrastructure teams to get systems to production readiness without cutting corners on either reliability or access control.

// research

Published research

IEEE WCNC 2025 · Dubai, UAE · March 2025
A Multi-Layered Zero Trust Microsegmentation Solution for Cloud-Native 5G & Beyond Networks
Chafika Benzaid, Nawal Guerd, Nour El Houda Rehouma, Khaled Zeraoulia, Tarik Taleb
  • Proposed THAALOUB, a Zero Trust framework delivering 3GPP-compliant, end-to-end security in cloud-native 5G/B5G networks.
  • Combined service mesh and CNI technologies for multi-layered microsegmentation and intent-based access control.
  • Validated experimentally, showing an improved security posture with minimal added latency or resource overhead.
Read the paper (PDF) →

This work grew out of my master's thesis, Design and Implementation of a Zero Trust Security Solution for 5G Networks, at USTHB — where I also built an automated deployment application for 5G infrastructure and cluster management, and implemented the resulting Zero Trust policies with Istio.
